## Top stories

### 1. Huawei Cloud: openPangu-2.1-Pro preset service goes live, output priced below the old short-request tier
- Sources: [Release bulletin](https://support.huaweicloud.com/bulletin-maas/bulletin-maas-0001.html); [Model list](https://support.huaweicloud.com/model-list-maas/model_list_0001.html); [Text-generation pricing](https://support.huaweicloud.com/price-maas/price-maas-0002.html); [Quick start](https://support.huaweicloud.com/bestpractice-maas/bestpractice_maas_0021_03.html)
- Type: Model release
- Rating: ★★★★☆
- Summary: Huawei Cloud MaaS lists the openPangu-2.1-Pro preset service in Southwest-Guiyang 1, at 0.016 yuan cached input, 0.8 yuan input, and 3.2 yuan output per million tokens.
- Key figure: 3.2 yuan | per million output tokens, below the 2.0-Pro short-request price

**Why it matters:** OpenPangu already had two rows on this price sheet. Version 20260630 of 2.0-Flash is 0.2 yuan cached input, 0.8 yuan input, and 1.6 yuan output. Version 20260730 of 2.0-Pro, when a request is under 32K tokens, is 0.8 yuan cached input, 3.2 yuan input, and 14.5 yuan output; at 32K or more, those three prices are 1.2, 4.8, and 17.6 yuan. Version 20261115 of 2.1-Pro is not split at 32K. Its three prices are 0.016, 0.8, and 3.2 yuan. The 14.5 yuan figure is 2.0-Pro's short-request output price, not an earlier price of 2.1-Pro. In the column for peak and off-peak pricing, both 2.1-Pro and 2.0-Pro say no. On the model list, both rows have a 512K context and a 500K max input. Max output is written as 384K instead of 128K, and max chain-of-thought as 256K instead of 64K.

**Key points:**
1. The release bulletin checks only the preset service. Public-pool and dedicated-pool deployment are both X, and the only region is Southwest-Guiyang 1. 2.0-Pro has the same checks on that bulletin. The quick start requires an account with real-name verification, then MaaS access authorization, then opening the preset service and creating an API key. The key-creation note says the function is available only in Southwest-Guiyang 1. The request uses the model parameter openpangu-2.1-pro. The chat URL is `/v2/chat/completions` on `api.modelarts-maas.com`, with an OpenAI-compatible `/openai/v1`.
2. On the model list, the default limit for 2.1-Pro is 2,000,000 tokens per minute and 100 requests per minute. 2.0-Pro is 1,000,000 tokens per minute and still 100 requests per minute. What doubled is the TPM quota. Deep thinking can be turned off. Function Call, prefix continuation, and prefix cache are already on the 2.0-Pro row.
3. The model list says 2.1 is a clear step up on agents, code generation, and throughput, and says it is strong at HarmonyOS app development and Ascend operator generation. The quick start says it is a clear step up from the previous generation. Neither page gives comparison scores. IT Home and National Business Daily both reported the listing on 15 November. The reports repeat the announcement. They are not an independent retest.

**What it means:** Huawei Cloud has put openPangu on the MaaS preset service in this order: 2.0-Flash on 30 June 2026, 2.0-Pro on 30 July, and now 2.1-Pro. The 2.0 weights are in the openpangu organization on Hugging Face, with creation dates matching the version dates. A listing of that organization and a search for openPangu-2.1 did not show 2.1. What a caller can match today is a lower Pro price and higher caps on output and chain-of-thought. On the same price sheet, GLM-5.3 output is 28 yuan. That locates the price. It does not say which model is better at code or agents. The default TPM moves from 1,000,000 to 2,000,000, while RPM stays at 100. That is not a measured doubling of throughput. If weights are published later the way 2.0 was, other inference stacks can talk about switching. Until then it is a metered API in Guiyang 1 that requires real-name verification and authorization.

**Risks and open questions:** "Clear step up" and the HarmonyOS and Ascend wording are Huawei Cloud's sentences on the model list and the quick start. The IT Home and National Business Daily reports are not a retest, and the price is not a measure of ability. The price page says the console is the price that applies. Cache is automatic on a shared prefix, the hit rate is not guaranteed, and a miss can raise time to first token. Failed generations are not billed. Parameter counts are not on the pages above.

1. **sarulab-speech · DialogueSidon2 separates a two-person recording in one forward pass**: The model card says a degraded two-person conversation comes back as two 24 kHz tracks. The weights and `ds2_infer.py`, which needs torch and torchaudio, can be downloaded under CC BY-NC 4.0. DialogueSidon, dated 28 March 2026, sampled latents with a diffusion model. On the authors' own table, Switchboard word error is 12.706 percent, against 14.432 percent for the previous release. On CallFriend, character error is higher for French, Japanese, and Spanish. It handles exactly two speakers. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/sarulab-speech/DialogueSidon2)
2. **Agnes AI · Agnes 2.5 Pro Theta weights are public**: The Hugging Face repository was created at 10:46 Beijing time on 15 November, under Apache-2.0. The model card lists 187,763,545,616 parameters. Five of the eight supplied comparisons are marked highest in that row. SciCode, HLE-Diamond, and GDP.pdf are not. The weight payload is about 349.7 GiB. The card calls four H200-class GPUs a starting point, and its environment check does not cover a full generation run. The same organization already had 2.5 Pro Alpha on 19 August. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/Agnes-AI/Agnes-2.5-Pro-Theta)
