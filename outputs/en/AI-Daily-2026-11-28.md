### 1. Kimi: Researchers can apply for up to $20,000 in API credits
- Sources: [Official page](https://www.kimi.ai/news/kimi-for-science-program); [Official account post](https://x.com/Kimi_Moonshot/status/2126154696947871760)
- Type: Industry
- Rating: ★★★☆☆
- Summary: Moonshot AI published Kimi for Science on the evening of November 26, opening applications to researchers in the natural sciences, engineering, and related interdisciplinary fields; selected projects can receive up to $20,000 in Kimi API credits, and the first round closes on December 8.
- Key figure: $20,000 | maximum API credits for a selected project

**Why it matters:** When labs give researchers access to models, the usual route is API credits or academic seats, not cash. OpenAI's Researcher Access Program is for applicants studying responsible deployment, risk, or societal impact; the official page sets a cap of $1,000 in API credits for publicly available models, valid for 12 months. OpenAI also has a ChatGPT seat program for academic researchers in science, mathematics, and engineering, but that offer does not include API credits. Kimi is pointing the application form at the natural sciences, engineering, and related interdisciplinary fields, and it sets the per-project cap at $20,000 in API credits (about 7 billion tokens), with technical support and early access to new models. The credits are for model usage, not cash funding.

**Key points:**
1. The official page's `datePublished` is 2026-11-26T13:05:36.017Z, which is 21:05:36 on November 26, Beijing time. The official account @Kimi_Moonshot posted at 2026-11-27T03:22:36.000Z (11:22:36 on November 27, Beijing time), writing "Starting today" and linking to the same application page.
2. Selected applicants can receive up to $20,000 in Kimi API credits (about 7 billion tokens, per the official page). The exact amount depends on research needs and the review. The official FAQ says the credits are for model usage, not cash funding. The first round closes on December 8, and more rounds will follow.
3. Applications are open to researchers and teams in the natural sciences, engineering, and related interdisciplinary fields. The official post says this includes PhD students, postdocs, and principal investigators (PIs). Credits are issued per research project and can be shared with collaborators. Selected projects also get technical support from the Kimi team, early access to new models, and a path to longer-term collaboration. The contact address is ai4science@moonshot.ai.

**What it means:** Trading API credits for scientific workloads and early feedback is not a new path: OpenAI already runs that model in the Researcher Access Program, with a $1,000 cap, quarterly reviews, and a focus on responsible AI and societal-impact research. What Kimi adds is a higher credit cap aimed at the natural sciences and engineering, plus a clear deadline for the first round. The questions to watch are how selective the review is, how much is actually granted, and whether this becomes a standing series of rounds.

**Risks and open questions:** Opening applications is not the same as receiving credits. The official FAQ says selection and support details are subject to the final review. The "about 7 billion tokens" figure is the official conversion of $20,000; this issue did not re-check it against a price list. The official page does not state the review cycle, the acceptance rate, or whether credits are released in stages.
