### 1. Google: The Gemini app can generate looping GIFs of about 3 seconds
- Sources: [Official account](https://x.com/GeminiApp/status/2123813017855103449); [Gemini Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16126339?hl=en); [Omni 1.2 Flash post](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/); [OpenAI changelog](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog)
- Type: Product update
- Rating: ★★★★☆
- Summary: The Gemini app can make looping GIFs of about 3 seconds with Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash. The help page requires a Google AI plan on a personal account, age 18 or older, and presence in a listed place. The feature is still rolling out.
- Key figure: ~3 sec | looping length on the help page

**Why it matters:** The official account's post has `created_at` 2026-11-20T16:17:36Z, 00:17 on November 21 in Beijing time. The post only says GIFs can be made with Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, from looping photo effects to memes, with smooth motion and consistent frames. Length, plan, age, and place are not in that post. They are on the help page for the same feature. The help page treats video and GIFs together: a personal account needs a Google AI plan to use the feature; a work or school account needs a qualifying Workspace license; people under 18 cannot use it. Creating a GIF also requires being in Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Japan, Korea, the UK, or the US. The page says the GIF ability is rolling out gradually and may not be available yet.

**Key points:**
1. The help page says a GIF is made from text, images, and/or video, runs about 3 seconds, loops, can be copied into documents, email, messages, and social apps, and can be downloaded as a `.gif`. The model named by the official account is Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash.
2. The earlier release of that model has the blog header Nov 05, 2026. `datePublished` is 2026-11-05T17:00:00+00:00, 01:00 on November 6 in Beijing time. The post says it is generally available on the Gemini API and in Gemini for business, with native video up to 15 seconds. Omni 1.1 stopped at 10 seconds. Duration can be chosen in one-second steps from 3 to 15 seconds. 720p video is $0.07 per second, 30% below Omni 1.1.
3. The OpenAI API changelog, under November 2026, says on the Nov 4 entry that image generation and editing for GPT-Image-2.6 Sunburst and Flare include animated GIFs. That entry has no clock time. The Gemini help page does not state a price per GIF in the Gemini app.

**What it means:** On the line of short GIFs, the OpenAI image API changelog already listed animated GIFs on November 4. The Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash post is dated November 5 on its header, puts callable video at 15 seconds, and prices 720p at $0.07 per second. What the Gemini app adds is a looping GIF of about 3 seconds, not a change of the in-app video cap to the 15 seconds in that post. The decision of whether to generate a GIF instead of searching for one depends on the help page's places, a Google AI plan, age 18, and whether the rollout has reached the account. The per-second video price on the API is not a per-GIF price.

**Risks and open questions:** The roughly 3-second length, the places, the plan, and the age come from the help page, not from the official post. The help page puts video and GIFs under one feature. A personal account needs a Google AI plan. The page does not separately say GIFs are available without that plan. The rollout is gradual, and the page says the ability may not be available yet. People under 18 cannot use it. The $0.07 figure from November 5 is the price per second of 720p video. This issue did not retest which accounts can actually see GIFs in the app.

### 2. OpenAI: ChatGPT Space starts rolling out to Plus users aged 18 and older
- Sources: [ChatGPT release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes); [Official account](https://x.com/ChatGPT/status/2123850912867471807)
- Type: Product update
- Rating: ★★★☆☆
- Summary: The release notes say ChatGPT Space is rolling out globally, starting that day, to Plus users aged 18 and older. People with Space access can build interactive pages and presentations together. Free accounts cannot open shared pages.
- Key figure: 18 | minimum age for this Plus rollout

**Why it matters:** In the same release notes, the September 29 entry gives Space to eligible Pro, Business, and Enterprise users, on the desktop app and the web. For accounts with access, it replaces Library. Projects stay separate. The October 16 slides entry is for Pro, Business, and Enterprise users who already have Space, on the web and the desktop app. The November 20 entry says that, starting that day, Space is rolling out globally to Plus users aged 18 and older. The official account's post has `created_at` 2026-11-20T18:48:11Z, 02:48 on November 21 in Beijing time. The text says Plus users can use Space, collaborate on interactive pages, slides, and sites, and edit on mobile.

**Key points:**
1. The November 20 entry says people with Space access can create interactive pages and presentations together, and that pages and slides can update automatically from connected apps and tools. Tagging ChatGPT in a comment asks for a change. A dot can be invited if there is one. The entry does not say Plus accounts receive a dot because of it.
2. Opening a shared page or presentation requires permission to view it and a sign-in on Plus, Pro, Business, or Enterprise with Space access. Free accounts cannot open shared pages or presentations. Public links are not available.
3. Space in the sidebar replaces Library. It is the home for pages, slides, images, and uploaded files. Creating and editing is listed for the web, the desktop app, iOS, and Android. The September 29 entry lists the desktop app and the web.

**What it means:** On the line of shared workspaces, September 29 is pages and files for Pro, Business, and Enterprise. October 16 is slides for that same group. Today the notes place it on Plus, and they list creating and editing on iOS and Android. The judgment is a step down the plan ladder, not a new kind of document. The people affected are Plus users aged 18 or older whose accounts have received the rollout. Free accounts still cannot open a page or presentation someone else shared. An account that does not yet see Space does not lose Library because this note exists.

**Risks and open questions:** "Starting today" and the rollout wording come from the November 20 release-notes entry. The entry does not say every Plus account can see Space in the sidebar the same day. The official post has a precise time. That release-notes entry does not. Inviting a dot requires already having one. The entry does not open dots to Plus. A shared page still requires the other person to have Space access. Public links are not available.
